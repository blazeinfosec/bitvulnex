  Trading-volume for fee tiers counts fills it should not. One side's
  cancelled orders still accrue toward the discount.
tier-2-verbose: |
  The fee-tier volume query filters out cancelled taker orders but
  forgets the maker side, so a filled-then-cancelled maker order still
  counts toward 30-day volume. Pair with self-trading: place a maker
  order, fill it from a second account, cancel the maker, and the trade
  row survives to inflate volume. A few hundred wash cycles reach a top
  fee tier at near-zero real cost.
