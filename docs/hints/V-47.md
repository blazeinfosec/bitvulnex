target: V-47
category: Business logic — premature fee refund
tier-1-basic: |
  When a withdrawal's fee is bumped down, the difference is refunded
  immediately — before the replacement transaction is known to confirm.
tier-2-verbose: |
  Bumping a broadcast withdrawal to a lower fee synchronously credits
  the fee difference back to available balance, with no watcher to claw
  it back if the replacement is dropped from the mempool. A low
  replacement fee that never confirms (or gets dropped by a follow-on
  bump) leaves the user holding the refund and possibly the original
  settlement. Looping low-fee bumps across many withdrawals drains the
  books slowly without any single anomaly.
