---
target: V-32
category: Crypto / Race condition
tier-1-basic: |
  A cancel handler and a concurrent fill don't share a transaction.
  Two passes through the path can produce more refund than the
  original lock.
tier-2-verbose: |
  The order cancel decrements locked balance and increments
  available based on a snapshot read taken outside the database
  transaction. A concurrent partial fill that completes between the
  read and the write makes the refund larger than what was actually
  locked. Race the cancel against a small market order on a quiet
  pair.
