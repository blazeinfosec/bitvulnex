category: Business logic — yield timing
tier-1-basic: |
  Interest is computed for the elapsed window, then split across whoever
  is supplying right now — including someone who only just joined.
tier-2-verbose: |
  The accrual worker computes interest from the borrow side across the
  whole window, but reads the supplier set AFTER and divides by current
  supply. Deposit a large amount into a high-utilization pool just
  before the periodic tick and you capture a share of yield earned while
  your capital was not at risk. Withdraw right after the tick. The
  pool's rate and last-accrual timing are inferable from public data, so
  you can front-run the tick.
