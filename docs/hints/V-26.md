category: Crypto / Business logic — limit window
tier-1-basic: |
  Daily withdrawal limits use a calendar-day bucket. The boundary
  between buckets is a wall-clock moment; two transactions on
  opposite sides of it both pass.
tier-2-verbose: |
  The limit ledger keys on userId, asset, and a UTC-truncated date.
  A withdrawal at 23:59:59 and another at 00:00:01 the next day are
  scored against different buckets even though they're seconds
  apart. Stage a tier-1 account near the boundary, drain the bucket,
  flip the date, drain again.
